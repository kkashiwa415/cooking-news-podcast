Regarding perpetual contracts—or "perps" as they're known in crypto—traders are expressing mixed feelings about these financial instruments. Perps are futures contracts with no expiration date that let traders take leveraged positions on cryptocurrency prices. They've become incredibly popular because they offer flexibility and the potential for significant profits.

But perps also carry substantial risks. They can amplify losses just as easily as they amplify gains. Traders are grappling with the reality that while perps are useful tools for sophisticated investors, they can also lead to catastrophic losses if positions aren't managed carefully. The debate in the crypto community right now is about how to harness the benefits of perps while building better guardrails against the kind of blowups that can wipe out retail traders.

For anyone trading crypto, understand that perps are powerful tools that require discipline and risk management. They're not inherently good or bad, but they're absolutely not for beginners.
