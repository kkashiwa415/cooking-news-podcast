Speaking of macro factors, the risk of US rate hikes is receding, and that's good news for crypto bulls. When rates are high or expected to rise, investors demand higher returns and tend to move away from riskier assets like bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies. When rate risk goes down, the math becomes more favorable for crypto. Lower rates mean the opportunity cost of holding an asset that doesn't pay interest decreases.
